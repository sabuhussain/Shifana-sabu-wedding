SHIFANA & SABU — PWA VERSION

Upload every file/folder in this ZIP to the root of your HTTPS website.

PWA:
- manifest.webmanifest enables Add to Home Screen / standalone app mode.
- sw.js provides offline caching after the first visit.
- icons/ contains home-screen icons.

WHATSAPP PREVIEW:
- preview.jpg is the share-card image.
- Open Graph tags are already added to index.html.
- For the preview to appear in WhatsApp, the site must be publicly reachable over HTTPS.
- After you know the final public URL/domain, adding an absolute og:url and absolute og:image URL is recommended.
